SK

Siddharth Kulkarni

Principal Investigator

ANRF Ramanujan Faculty Fellow, CSIR-Centre for Cellular and Molecular Biology · Assistant Professor, Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR)

I am fascinated by biodiversity and by what keeps it going. My work asks why some branches of the tree of life are staggeringly diverse while their close relatives, of exactly the same age, are not.

Before starting the lab I completed a Ph.D. with Gustavo Hormiga at George Washington University on the spider tree of life and the miniature orb-weavers, and a postdoc with Prashant Sharma at the University of Wisconsin-Madison on arachnid phylogenomics and genome architecture. Along the way I have described new species, three new families, and a chromosome-level genome or two.

Outside the lab I coordinate SpiderIndia, a community science network of more than 8,000 people across South Asia. Much of what we know about where Indian spiders actually live comes from them.
